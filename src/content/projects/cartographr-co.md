Cartographr Co. was an e-commerce brand that I ran with my best friend from mid 2022 until the end of 2025. We first focused on cartography based art, which we ourselves designed and later expanded into interieur deco items, like lamps. The brand did well in good times, reaching up to 5-digit revenue months. Eventually it became really tough to stay profitable, since we operated in the premium print sector and margins kept getting thinner and thinner due to rising material costs etc. Besides that, my friend and I shifted our focusses on new things and our individual paths, which is why we decided to close the brand end 2025.
